# Evidence and limits

This Skill combines reader-centered organization, explicit reasoning, and comparison with source commitments. The guidance and examples are original. The sources below inform decisions; their text, examples, checklist structures, and software are not reproduced or translated into this package.

## Reader needs and explanation

The [International Plain Language Federation's ISO overview](https://www.iplfederation.org/iso-standard/) describes relevant, findable, understandable, and usable information as the reader outcomes behind ISO 24495-1. That public overview informs the final reader judgment in `xc-writing`. The full standard was not consulted; this Skill makes no ISO conformity or certification claim. The overview's discussion of science writing also does not establish that a single style suits every specialist journal.

[Google's audience guidance](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/audience), [word guidance](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/words), and [paragraph guidance](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/paragraphs) support attending to prior knowledge, stable terminology, and connected explanation. [GSA's writing guidance](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/writing) informs attention to actors and nominalized actions. Their useful principles are applied with judgment: this Skill imposes neither a paragraph size nor an absolute ban on passive voice.

[W3C's cognitive usability guidance](https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/) informed nearby explanations and reducing unnecessary memory demands; only the overview and relevant patterns were consulted. It is supplemental guidance, not a WCAG conformance test. Reader needs vary, and guidance for a particular population cannot justify assumptions about an individual reader.

The [PLOS discussion of scientific paper structure](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619), [MIT/Broad's introduction guidance](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/broad/commkit/journal-article-introduction/), and [Diátaxis on explanation](https://diataxis.fr/explanation/) inform the relationship among context, evidence, and reasoning. They do not require a conversational answer to follow a journal-paper template. [The Chinese technical style guide](https://github.com/ruanyf/document-style-guide) informed attention to natural word order and clear reference; its numerical style preferences are not adopted.

## Presentation and evaluation

[Nielsen Norman Group's account of web reading](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-users-read-on-the-web/) and [progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) inform visible entry points and optional detail. Web scanning findings do not establish how everyone reads a sustained design argument. Essential exceptions remain beside their claims rather than behind optional navigation.

[GSA's testing guidance](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/test) informs asking readers to explain or use information instead of merely rating how much they like it. This Skill's semantic examples support bounded review. They are not controlled experiments or evidence that the Skill improves human performance.

Only the abstract and bibliographic record of [Lost in the Middle](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/) were consulted. The reported sensitivity to information position motivates checking relevant facts in long inputs. It does not prove a cause of writing difficulty in a current model or show that this method fixes long-context performance.

## Other writing Skills considered

[i-have-adhd](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd) offers action-support choices; [humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) examines formulaic prose; [Anthropic's skill-creator](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/skill-creator) offers guidance on Skill design and evaluation. They informed the comparison of alternatives, without importing their instructions, examples, persistent modes, word lists, or programs. This Skill does not infer a diagnosis or describe ordinary technical readers as a clinical group.

Research can justify a design choice without demonstrating its effect. Source attribution does not itself establish permission to reproduce material, and unknown reuse conditions are not permission. If future revisions incorporate external wording, structure, or examples, reassess attribution and license obligations before distribution. Automated similarity checks can aid that review but cannot prove originality.
