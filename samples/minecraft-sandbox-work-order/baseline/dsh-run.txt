Built the complete game at `index.html` in the current working directory.

**Included**
- Deterministic procedural terrain from a numeric seed, with grass, dirt, stone, sand, caves, trees, and bedrock.
- Player movement, gravity, jumping, coyote time, and solid-block collision.
- Left-click mining/hold-to-mine with reach limits.
- Right-click placement with face detection, empty-cell checks, support checks, inventory counts, and player-overlap prevention.
- 8-slot hotbar with number-key/wheel selection and a prominent gold selected state.
- Smooth camera clamped to world bounds.
- Autosaved world, player position, inventory, and selection in `localStorage`.
- Seeded New World dialog, Help dialog, responsive HUD, and offline-only single-file implementation.

**Controls**
- `A/D` or `←/→` — move
- `W`, `Space`, or `↑` — jump
- Left-click — mine
- Right-click — build
- `1–8` or mouse wheel — select block
- `H` — help

Verified in Chrome via automated interaction tests: movement, jumping, mining, placing, inside-player placement rejection, deterministic seed generation, persistence across reload, responsive layouts, no console errors, and 60 FPS.
