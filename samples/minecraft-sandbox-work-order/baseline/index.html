Field notesopen this page
Two useful habits: right-click a block face to place beside it, and try not to wall yourself in. Blocks cannot be placed where you are standing, and bedrock at the bottom of the world is unbreakable.
Autosaves locally. No account, no cloud, no way out except digging.
