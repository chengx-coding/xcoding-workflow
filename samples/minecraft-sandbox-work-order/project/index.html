Materials

    Field notes

    Reach is five blocks. A placed block needs a solid neighbour, a clear cell, and a body-sized gap — the explorer cannot be built into a wall.

    Mined blocks refill the hotbar counts. The camera follows your explorer and stops at the edges of the world. Progress is written to this browser only.

    No network, no libraries, no build step. Open the file and play.
