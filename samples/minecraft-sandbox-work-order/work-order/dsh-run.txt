## Delivered

`project/index.html` — **Terra**, a playable offline 2D Minecraft-style sandbox:

- Seeded terrain with grass, dirt, stone, wood, leaves, sand, planks and glass; hills, trees, caves, ore speckles and flowers.
- Gravity, left/right movement, jumping with coyote time, and full solid-block collision.
- Left-click mining within five blocks, right-click supported placement, player-body refusal, particles, ghost previews and synthesized sound.
- Eight-slot hotbar with number keys 1–8, wheel cycling and a raised gold selected state.
- Smooth camera clamped to world bounds, layered sky/parallax, textured blocks and an animated explorer.
- `localStorage` save/restore of seed, edits, position, hotbar and inventory, with a visible save indicator.
- In-page field guide (`H`/`P`/`Esc`), seed generation, randomiser and two-step reset.
- Controls documented in `README.md` and the in-page guide.
