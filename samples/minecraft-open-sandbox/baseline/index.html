Your first day

  1. Punch a tree. Hold left-click on a trunk until it pops.
  2. Open your pockets. Press E and craft planks from logs, then sticks, then a crafting table.
  3. Make tools. A wooden pickaxe needs 3 planks and 2 sticks. Place the table to unlock better recipes nearby.
  4. Dig down. Coal feeds torches. Iron makes stronger tools and swords.
  5. Build a shelter. Place blocks with right-click. Zombies roam after sunset.
  6. Eat and rest. Apples and cooked pork fill your hunger. Stand still to heal when well fed.

Good to know

  • Your world autosaves every few seconds and when you pause or leave.
  • Torches light caves. Monsters spawn in the dark—light your home.
  • Ores need better pickaxes: stone for iron, iron for gold and diamond.
  • Hold Shift while using a table or furnace to place a block instead.
  • Use the recipe cards in your inventory; ingredients are pulled automatically.
Everything is generated and saved on this device.
