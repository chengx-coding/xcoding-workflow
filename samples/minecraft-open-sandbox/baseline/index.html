The old ward

Gather wood, build a workbench, and follow the field notes in the top right. Coal hides in the shallow dark. Iron waits deeper and needs a stone pickaxe. The old Warden's Lantern is the last step.

Torches keep the hollows at bay. Placed blocks stay exactly where you leave them.

Field notes

  • Tools wear out but stone and iron tiers last much longer.
  • Choose the right tool: a pickaxe for rock, an axe for wood, a sword for trouble.
  • Right-click a solid block to place your block on top of it.
  • Progress saves on this browser automatically and when you pause.
  • No internet, install, or account is needed. Everything is generated locally.
