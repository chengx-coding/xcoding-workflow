    First steps

    1. Hold left mouse on a tree to gather logs.
    2. Craft planks and a crafting table in your pack.
    3. Place the table, then craft a wooden pickaxe.
    4. Mine stone, upgrade to stone, and light a cave.

    Reading the world

    Hard blocks need better pickaxes. Coal needs wood; iron needs stone. Place torches on solid ground. Your surface map shows your position across the valley.
