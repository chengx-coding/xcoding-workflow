Progress

  1. Punch a tree to collect logs.
  2. Craft planks and sticks, then a crafting table.
  3. Place the table and press E to craft a pickaxe.
  4. Mine stone and coal, then build a furnace.
  5. Smelt iron, craft better tools, and light caves with torches.
  6. At night, fight or shelter. Keep an eye on hearts and hunger.
