    How to play

    1. Hold left click on a tree to mine wood. Tool speed matters: stone needs a pickaxe.
    2. Open Crafting (C) and turn wood into planks, sticks and a pickaxe.
    3. Pick a block with 1–9, then right click to place it. Blocks need a neighbour.
    4. Press B to review the before/after ghosts of what you changed.
    5. Torches push back the dark. Your world saves automatically; F2 saves now.

    Saving

    Progress saves to this browser. You can also export a save file from the menu.
